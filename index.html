FAQ

Straight answers

Can it book assessments and trial lessons?

Yes. It checks live availability in Google or Outlook Calendar, books the right session type and texts a confirmation.

Does it promise better grades?

No. It describes your programs as you wrote them and never promises scores or results.

Will it share a student’s progress with whoever asks?

No. Questions about progress, grades or attendance become a message for your team, who can verify who is asking.

Can it handle online tutoring enquiries?

Yes. It explains how your online sessions work and books them the same way as in-person ones.

What about students under 18?

Add a rule that a parent or guardian’s name and number are required before booking, and it will ask for them.

Does it integrate with my tutoring software?

It books into Google Calendar, Outlook or a built-in calendar, and sends leads to other tools via Zapier, Make, n8n or webhooks.
